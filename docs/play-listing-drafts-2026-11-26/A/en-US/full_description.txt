Meeting Mind: AI meeting notes in many languages

Record a meeting, a lecture or an interview and get a transcript, a summary and a list of action items. Meeting Mind is free: no card and no subscription.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
- Record in person with your phone's microphone.
- Send the Meeting Mind bot to a Zoom, Google Meet or Microsoft Teams meeting. The bot joins as a visible participant named Meeting Mind. Bot time has a monthly limit.
- Get a summary, key points and action items for each recording.
- Search your recordings and ask questions about a meeting.
- See who said what: transcripts label the speakers, and you can rename them.
- Use the web app at meetingmind.org with the same account. You can also upload audio files you already have there.

TRANSCRIPTS IN 30 LANGUAGES
Meeting Mind detects the spoken language and writes the transcript in it: Bulgarian, Catalan, Chinese (Mandarin), Czech, Danish, Dutch, English, Finnish, French, German, Greek, Hindi, Hungarian, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Lithuanian, Malay, Norwegian, Polish, Portuguese, Russian, Slovak, Spanish, Swedish, Thai, Turkish, Ukrainian, and Vietnamese.
- One main language is detected for each recording.
- For an online meeting you can also choose its language before the bot joins.

SUMMARY LANGUAGE
Summaries are written in English unless you choose another language. When you record or upload on the web app, you can pick one of 15 summary languages.

THE APP IN YOUR LANGUAGE
The Android app is available in 27 languages. You can change the app language in Settings.

AI COACH
AI Coach live coaching during bot meetings is optional and uses separate credits. Everything else above is free.

GOOD TO KNOW
- You are responsible for telling people you are recording and for following the recording laws where you are. Some places require everyone's consent.
- Transcript quality depends on the audio: clear speech and little background noise work best.
- Very short recordings do not get a summary. Say a little more, or upload longer audio on the web app.
- Recordings are processed by the AI and transcription services listed in our privacy policy.
