Meeting Mind: free AI meeting notes

Record a meeting, a lecture or a phone call and get a transcript, a summary and a list of action items. Meeting Mind is free: no card and no subscription.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
- Record in person with your phone's microphone.
- Make phone calls from the app and have them recorded and transcribed.
- Send the Meeting Mind bot to a Zoom, Google Meet or Microsoft Teams meeting. The bot joins as a visible participant named Meeting Mind. Bot time has a monthly limit.
- Get a summary, key points and action items for each recording.
- Search your recordings and ask questions about a meeting.
- Use the web app at meetingmind.org with the same account. You can also upload audio files you already have there.

AI COACH
AI Coach live coaching during bot meetings is optional and uses separate credits. Everything else above is free.

GOOD TO KNOW
- You are responsible for telling people you are recording and for following the recording laws where you are. Some places require everyone's consent.
- Very short recordings do not get a summary. Say a little more, or upload longer audio on the web app.
- Recordings are processed by the AI and transcription services listed in our privacy policy.
