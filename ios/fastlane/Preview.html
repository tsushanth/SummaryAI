Meeting Mind - AI Meeting Recorder & Transcription

Transform how you capture and remember every meeting, lecture, and conversation. Meeting Mind uses advanced AI to record, transcribe, and summarize your audio so you never miss important details again.

KEY FEATURES

Instant Transcription
Record any meeting, lecture, or conversation and get accurate transcriptions powered by cutting-edge AI. Support for multiple speakers with automatic speaker detection.

Smart Summaries
Don't have time to read through lengthy transcripts? Get concise AI-generated summaries that capture the key points, decisions, and important moments.

Action Items
Never forget follow-ups again. Meeting Mind automatically extracts action items and to-dos from your conversations, helping you stay on top of your commitments.

Searchable Archive
All your recordings are organized and searchable. Quickly find that important discussion from weeks ago by searching through transcripts.

Record Anywhere
Use Meeting Mind for business meetings and team calls, lectures and classes, interviews and consultations, personal voice memos, and phone calls.

Sync Across Devices
Your recordings and transcripts sync securely to the cloud, accessible from any device.

HOW IT WORKS

Tap to start recording
Meeting Mind captures high-quality audio
AI processes and transcribes your recording
Review your transcript, summary, and action items
Search and reference anytime
